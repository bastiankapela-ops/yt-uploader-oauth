Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026

Video Essay Studio is a personal automation tool used solely by the account owner.

Data we access

  • Google / YouTube OAuth credentials needed to upload videos to channels the owner controls
  • Video files, titles, and descriptions created on the owner’s local machine

How data is used

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner’s computer and used only to upload and schedule videos to their YouTube channels. Data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties, except as required to communicate with Google/YouTube APIs.

Retention

Tokens and media remain on the owner’s local machine until deleted by the owner. YouTube retains uploaded videos according to the owner’s YouTube account settings and Google’s policies.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the repository owner via GitHub: bastiankapela-ops.
